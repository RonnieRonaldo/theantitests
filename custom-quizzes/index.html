THE PRACTICAL PART

Custom quiz questions.
Answered before the quiz.

What does US$200 include?

One custom online quiz with ten short A/B/C questions, three result profiles, a personalized title, a dedicated page in The Anti-Tests style, one consolidated revision round and 90 days of hosting from launch. It uses an existing design rather than a separately designed website. Extra questions, further revisions or extended hosting require a separate quote.

Is it a live team game with a presenter?

No. This is an individual personality quiz that you share through a link. Participants can take it at different times. There is no shared login session, host dashboard, leaderboard or synchronized round of questions.

Can this be a team personality quiz for colleagues?

Yes, as light entertainment for your group. It is not an employee assessment or a way to evaluate performance. The organizer does not receive a report of individual answers or a results dashboard. People can share their own profiles if they want to.

Is the dedicated page private?

It is accessible to anyone who has the link, and is not password protected. Custom pages will be kept out of the public quiz directory and marked not to be indexed by search engines; that is not an access restriction. Use material you are comfortable placing on a link-accessible webpage.

What should I send, and what should I leave out?

Send friendly anecdotes, recognizable habits, the occasion and your preferred tone. Leave out confidential company information, private health details and jokes that single someone out in a hurtful way. You review the wording before launch.

How soon can it be ready?

Tell me your preferred date when you enquire. I confirm availability and a delivery date before work begins. The timing depends on a clear brief and your feedback; sending an enquiry does not reserve a date.

What happens after 90 days?

The hosting period ends and the custom quiz page is taken offline. If you want it available longer, ask about an extension before the period ends. Permanent hosting and a separate domain are not part of this package.
