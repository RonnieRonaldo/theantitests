Perfectionism self-reflection test: The Great Anti-Perfectionism Test

You rewrote a two-line message four times. Then apologised for sending it. Excellent work. No notes. Except, naturally, your own.

This reflective perfectionism test explores overthinking, people-pleasing, procrastination, constant self-editing and the awkward business of letting good enough stay good enough.

Read fifteen scenes from The Great Anti-Perfectionism Test by Simon Star and choose the closest A/B/C response. The scenes use the book’s protective-pattern lens; their statements about childhood and safety are not facts established about you. If a scene does not fit, choose the closest reaction without treating its backstory as your own.

This is the general edition. For the pressures of parenting, try the Parent Edition.

15 questions · 3 reflective profiles · Around 20–30 minutes. Original book scenes and A/B/C branches, with adapted online profile summaries. For self-reflection, not diagnosis, trauma assessment or treatment. Progress is saved in this browser when storage is available.
