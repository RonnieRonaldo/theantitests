Funny family personality quiz: The Great Anti-Family Quiz

You came for dinner. You left with unsolicited advice, a printer problem and a strong opinion about somebody else’s potato salad.

This sarcastic family personality quiz explores awkward family gatherings, nosy relatives, sibling comparisons and the group chat that apparently requires round-the-clock staffing. Fifteen scenes. Three familiar ways of contributing to the problem.

Adapted from The Great Anti-Family Book by Simon Star, these new A/B/C questions ask how you react. Answer as yourself, not as the remarkably reasonable person you become when describing your relatives to friends.

15 questions · 3 satirical profiles · Around 8–12 minutes. A new quiz adaptation of the book’s fifteen portraits. For entertainment and self-reflection. Progress is saved in this browser when storage is available.
