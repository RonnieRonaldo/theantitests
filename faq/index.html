REASONABLE QUESTIONS. MOSTLY.

A few answers.
No personality required.

Are these personality quizzes free?

Yes. The published quizzes are free to take, with no account or payment needed to see your result. A personalized quiz made for your own group is a separate paid service.

How do my A, B and C answers become a profile?

Each answer contributes to one of three profiles. The profile with the most points determines your result. In the Christmas quiz, answers are matched to profiles by their meaning, so the same letter does not always represent the same profile. If there is a tie, you choose between the tied profiles. There are no correct answers to memorize. Your contradictions have already done enough preparation.

How long does a test take?

These are story-led quizzes, so allow around 20–30 minutes for the Anti-Consumption Test and 25–35 minutes for the Parent Edition, depending on your reading pace. Those two contain 15 questions each. The Anti-Social Media Test also has 15 questions; allow around 15–25 minutes. Not a Christmas Quiz has 28 questions across 14 chapters; allow around 25–35 minutes. The Anti-Routine Test has 15 questions; allow around 10–15 minutes. The Anti-Success Test has 15 questions; allow around 15–20 minutes. The Anti-Therapy Quiz has 15 questions; allow around 15–20 minutes. The Anti-Motivation Test has 15 questions; allow around 15–20 minutes. The Anti-Friend Quiz has 15 questions; allow around 8–12 minutes. The Anti-Couple Quiz has 15 questions; allow around 8–12 minutes. Each person answers individually; there is no compatibility score. The Anti-Work Quiz has 15 questions; allow around 8–12 minutes. It is workplace satire, not a career aptitude or burnout test. Take your time; self-recognition is rarely improved by a stopwatch.

Can I leave and come back?

Yes, if browser storage is available. Your progress is saved in that browser on that device. Return to the same quiz to continue. Clearing site data, switching devices or using a different browser will not bring your saved answers along.

Is this a scientific personality assessment?

No. These tests are for entertainment and self-reflection, not a clinical diagnosis, a validated psychological assessment or professional advice. The Parent Edition is not a parenting grade. Nobody here is handing out gold stars for a perfectly packed lunch.

Do I have to accept analytics to play?

No. Accepting or rejecting optional analytics does not change the quiz or your result. You can change your choice through “Analytics settings” in the footer. Read our privacy explanation for the details.

Can I suggest a quiz or request one for my group?

Yes. Suggest a topic through the contact section, or explore a custom online personality quiz for a company celebration, birthday or family gathering. Everyone follows the link and takes it at their own pace.
