BEHIND THE ANTI-TESTS

Special tests.
Suspiciously familiar people.

Some personality quizzes ask for your favorite color and announce that you are a loyal dolphin. Lovely. But the dolphin has never bought storage boxes to organize things it should not have bought in the first place.

The Anti-Tests is for adults who would like their self-discovery with a little more recognition and considerably less motivational wallpaper. These unusual personality quizzes explore everyday habits, social pressure and the stories we tell ourselves to make it all sound intentional.

In The Great Anti-Consumption Test, shopping habits, impulse buying and consumer culture receive the attention normally reserved for a delivery notification. In the Parent Perfectionism Test, the tone is gentler: a little space to notice the pressure to be a flawless parent while being an actual human.

Read a situation. Choose A, B or C. Your answers lead to a personality profile. Expect dry humor, cynical observations, occasional dark humor and the uncomfortable pleasure of thinking, “Unfortunately, yes.” The parenting test brings more warmth than bite; not every subject needs a roast.

Created by Simon Star, this growing collection brings the Anti-series into interactive online quizzes. Two tests are ready now; more are on the way, including a Christmas personality quiz for people whose festive enthusiasm requires negotiation.

REASONABLE QUESTIONS. MOSTLY.

A few answers.
No personality required.

Are these personality quizzes free?

Yes. The published quizzes are free to take, with no account or payment needed to see your result. A personalized quiz made for your own group is a separate paid service.

How do my A, B and C answers become a profile?

Each answer contributes to one of three profiles. Your most frequent answer type determines your result. If there is a tie, you choose between the tied profiles. There are no correct answers to memorize. Your contradictions have already done enough preparation.

How long does a test take?

These are story-led quizzes, so allow around 20–30 minutes for the Anti-Consumption Test and 25–35 minutes for the Parent Edition, depending on your reading pace. Both contain 15 questions. Take your time; self-recognition is rarely improved by a stopwatch.

Can I leave and come back?

Yes, if browser storage is available. Your progress is saved in that browser on that device. Return to the same quiz to continue. Clearing site data, switching devices or using a different browser will not bring your saved answers along.

Is this a scientific personality assessment?

No. These tests are for entertainment and self-reflection, not a clinical diagnosis, a validated psychological assessment or professional advice. The Parent Edition is not a parenting grade. Nobody here is handing out gold stars for a perfectly packed lunch.

Do I have to accept analytics to play?

No. Accepting or rejecting optional analytics does not change the quiz or your result. You can change your choice through “Analytics settings” in the footer. Read our privacy explanation for the details.

Can I suggest a quiz or request one for my group?

Yes. Suggest a topic through the contact section, or explore a custom online personality quiz for a company celebration, birthday or family gathering. Everyone follows the link and takes it at their own pace.
