BEHIND THE ANTI-TESTS

Life is absurd.
Take it personally.

The Anti-Tests brings Simon Star’s Anti-series into a collection of story-led quizzes for adults. Consumer pressure, impossible expectations, compulsory festive joy: ordinary life provides plenty of material.

Expect dry humor, occasional dark humor, and situations that feel a little too familiar. Some tests are sharper; others take a softer approach. Each gives you room to read, choose, and recognise something of yourself.
