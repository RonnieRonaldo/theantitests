Privacy & analytics.

You can use the quizzes whether you accept or reject analytics.

Optional Google Analytics

If you select “Accept analytics”, this website loads Google Analytics 4 to help us understand visits and how people use the site. Google may process cookie identifiers, pages visited, device and browser information, approximate location and interactions. Analytics cookies can recognise a browser across visits.

We do not load the Google Analytics script before you accept. Advertising storage and advertising personalisation consent stay off. We do not add your individual A/B/C choices or profile text as analytics event parameters. Page activity can still show which quiz you visit.

You can reject analytics or change your choice using “Analytics settings” at the bottom of any page. Withdrawing permission stops further analytics collection through our tag and removes its accessible first-party analytics cookies. It does not erase data already collected by Google.

For more about Google’s processing, read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your quiz progress

Quiz answers and progress are stored locally in your browser so you can return to a test. Each quiz uses separate saved progress. There is no quiz account and the quiz engine does not upload your answer list to a server. Your saved progress remains until you clear this site’s browser data or replace it by starting a new test.

Remembering your choice

We remember your analytics choice in local storage for up to 180 days. If your browser blocks storage, the choice may not persist between pages. Deleting this site’s browser data clears your saved quiz progress and analytics choice.

Sharing a result

Sharing is your choice. The share button prepares your profile name and a link to the quiz for the service you select. The link contains no answers or personal result. You can also copy the text yourself.

Website delivery

This website is hosted by Cloudflare. Loading a page involves technical request data, such as an IP address, being processed to deliver and protect the website. Optional analytics is separate from this website delivery.

Contact enquiries

When you submit the contact form, your name, email address, selected topic and message are sent to Web3Forms to deliver your enquiry. We use them to reply and discuss your request. The service also processes technical request information to operate the form and help prevent spam. See the Web3Forms privacy policy. The form does not save a draft in browser storage. Please do not include sensitive personal information, passwords or payment card details. Sending a message does not require accepting optional analytics.
